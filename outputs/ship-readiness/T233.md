### 2. Selecting and creating a trick — passed

- Selecting: Big cup and Base cup were selected during onboarding's trick-selection step and appeared correctly as "2 in rotation" on `/app/tricks` and in the Sesh trick list.
- Creating: used "Define new trick" → "Sequence" to create a custom sequence ("T233 test sequence") with a `customText` step ("Custom warmup spin") and a second `customText` step ("Cooldown flourish"), added to focused tricks. It appeared immediately in Tricks as "3 in rotation" with a "Custom sequence" tag, and its step names rendered correctly during the later Sesh run without re-resolving from any source trick (per the stored-snapshot invariant).

### 3. Timed Sesh, pause/resume, and backgrounding past the deadline — passed

Started a 1-minute timed Sesh on Big cup. Countdown (3-2-1) → active state showed `TIME REMAINING 00:57` almost immediately. Logged one lace (`Laced` → "First lace ever" toast, lace timeline entry `#01 00:09`). Paused at `00:45`; waited 3s while paused and confirmed the displayed remaining time did not tick down (still `00:45`) — pause correctly stops the clock. Resumed, timer continued from `00:41`.

To test backgrounding: opened a second, unrelated foreground browser tab (putting the Sesh tab in the background) and issued four consecutive 10s waits (40s total) targeted at the backgrounded Sesh tab, then re-fronted it. Result: `SESH COMPLETE` screen for Big cup, `01:00 TOTAL TIME`, `1 LACES`, average/fastest pace `00:09` — i.e., completion fired exactly once, with a sensible summary, and the recorded duration matches the planned 60s deadline rather than any backgrounded-clock drift.

Checked `guest:daily-dama:practice-history` in `localStorage` immediately after: exactly one session record for this run (`durationMs: 60000, laceCount: 1, plannedDurationMs: 60000`) — no duplicate history entries from the background/foreground transition.

### 4. Open-ended Sesh and early end — passed

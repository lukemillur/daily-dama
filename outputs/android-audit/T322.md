## Failure detail (case 12/13)

With a text field focused (`mInputShown=true`) the activity window does not resize or pan (`InsetsSource type=ime`). The form scroll view is already at maximum scroll, so Save/Cancel stay under the keyboard:

| Screen | Viewport | IME top | Save bounds | Cancel bounds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standalone new trick | 1080x2424 | y 1541 | [58,1849][1022,1996] | [58,2022][1022,2169] |
| Sequence new trick (and edit forms share the layout) | 1080x2424 | y 1541 | [58,1849][1022,1996] | same |
| Standalone new trick | 720x1280 | y 699 | [39,896][682,994] | [39,1012][682,1110] |
| Sequence new trick | 720x1280 | y 699 | [39,907][682,1005] | [39,1023][682,1121] |
| Note form | 720x1280 | y 699 | [39,827][682,925] (not scrollable) | [39,943][682,1041] |
| Goal form | 720x1280 | y 699 | [39,824][682,922] | [39,940][682,1038] |

Further swipes did not move the bounds. The note form's Save is above the keyboard at 1080x2424 (1245-1392; 1334-1481 in edit). Workaround observed: dismissing the keyboard with Back makes the buttons visible and tappable (used to save and cancel the fixtures), so no data was blocked; the failure is the reachability requirement. At 720x1280 the sequence form's `Search existing tricks` field itself sits behind the keyboard when focused.

Follow-up: **T325** (keep Save/Cancel and the focused field above the Android keyboard). T322 stays Blocked until the keyboard criterion is reverified at both viewports.

## Failure detail

### Case 7/8: Feedback form (`src/features/feedback/components/feedback-screen.tsx`)

The form uses a plain `ScrollView` (not `KeyboardAwareScroll`). With the Message field focused the content does not move.

| Viewport | IME top | Message field | Send feedback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1080x2424 | y 1541 | [65,1242][1015,1611] (partly under IME) | [58,1697][1022,1844] (under IME) |
| 720x1280 | y 699 | [44,753][678,998] (focused field hidden under IME) | [39,1056][682,1154] (under IME) |

Swipes above the keyboard did not change the bounds. Dismissing the keyboard with Back made Send tappable (not pressed). At 720x1280 the focused Message field itself is hidden while typing. Same family as T325, which only covered `KeyboardAwareScroll` screens.

Passes in case 8: Profile streak field (Save [58,1132][256,1273] above IME 1541 at 1080x2424; Save [39,417][171,512] above 699 at 720x1280; also at font 2.0), Profile hex field (Save reachable by scrolling), standalone new-trick form (Save/Cancel [58,1159..1479] above IME at 1080x2424; reachable by scrolling above y 699 at 720x1280, Cancel tapped with IME open returned to Tricks).

### Case 7: Feedback return lands on Home

Profile > Send feedback (`router.push("/feedback")`) > Android Back goes to Home, with Profile not selected. Reproduced twice.

### Case 9: Largest font scale (2.0)

- Preset labels wrap mid-word: `DEFAU/LT`, `ORAN/GE`, `YELLO/W`, `VIOLE/T` at both viewports and `INDIG/O` at 720x1280. Labels remain present but are not legible as words.
- Bottom tab icons are clipped by the labels (Home, Sesh and Profile icons cut off) at both viewports; the five labels themselves fit without truncation.
- Streak field and Save, Send feedback, Sign up/Sign in, Kentei button, picker and hex fields are reachable by scrolling and not truncated.
- At font 1.0 neither viewport showed clipped labels; the five-tab bar fits at 720x1280.

### TalkBack (case 10): BLOCKED

TalkBack was enabled and its Display speech output overlay worked (verified with a screenshot of spoken text). Only double-tap activation worked from adb: it activated the Sesh tab and moved focus to "START A SESH". Explore-by-touch (`input tap`, long press), single-finger swipes (`input swipe`, `touchscreen swipe`, `motionevent`) and Alt+Right did not move TalkBack focus in the app, so spoken tab names/selection, checked announcements, field/error announcements and focus return could not be observed. No TalkBack check is recorded as passed.

Static observations from the accessibility tree (not a substitute for spoken evidence):

- Tab buttons expose `content-desc` ", Home" etc. and `selected=true` on the active tab; the tab icon child has an empty description.
- Preset buttons: `Red theme preset`, with `selected` state; focus checkboxes expose `Big cup, selected` with `checked=true`.
- Fields expose labels (`Streak threshold in minutes`, `Background color hex`, `Name`, `Message`, `Trick name`).
- `TextField` renders the error as a plain `Text` with no live region and no association with the input (`src/components/ui/text-field.tsx`), so an announcement of the streak error is not guaranteed.
- Color spectrum surfaces expose labels (`... saturation and lightness`, `... hue`) but no adjustable action; the typed hex field is the alternative.

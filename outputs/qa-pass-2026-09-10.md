## Findings

### [severity: high] Skipping the "get ready" countdown for a new trick can log a spurious lace at 00:00

- What I did: Started an open-ended multi-trick sesh (Big cup... actually "ZZZ QA Unranked Trick" then "ZZZ QA Test Sequence"). Tapped "Next trick" to advance from trick 1 to trick 2 (the sequence), then clicked "Skip" on the "Get ready" countdown for trick 2.
- What happened: Immediately after skip, the active-practice screen for trick 2 showed "Laced. 1" with a lace timeline entry "#01 00:00 +00:00" — before I had tapped the Laced button myself. At sesh completion, "ZZZ QA Test Sequence" showed 2 laces even though I only intentionally tapped Laced once during that trick's active phase.
- What should happen: Laces should only be recorded from explicit user taps on the Laced control during active practice (per AGENTS.md, the Sesh reducer is the authority for countdown/active/completed transitions, and timestamps should be passed through actions rather than derived from ambient state). A lace at elapsed 00:00 immediately following a Skip tap strongly suggests the Skip control's tap is bleeding through to the Laced button once the view transitions from countdown to active, most likely because both controls render in the same screen position across the transition frame.
- Console/network evidence: None (no console error — this is a UI/interaction-layer defect, not a crash). Reproduced once; recommend re-verifying with a slower manual click (separate taps in time) to confirm the exact mechanism (click coordinate reuse vs. a genuine auto-registration on trick-advance).

### [severity: high] Nested `<button>` elements in Goal rows — invalid HTML / accessibility violation

- What I did: Added a lace-count goal to "Big cup" via Tricks → Goals for Big cup → Add goal, saved it, then viewed both the per-trick Goals page and the aggregate "Trick goals" overview page.
- What happened: Console logged, on every page that renders a `GoalRow` (per-trick Goals view, aggregate Trick goals view, and again on subsequent navigations that re-render it): `In HTML, %s cannot be a descendant of <%s>. This will cause a hydration error.` and `<%s> cannot contain a nested %s.` The stack trace shows a "Delete goal" `Pressable`/`<button>` nested inside a "Practice Big cup" `Pressable`/`<button>`.
- What should happen: Interactive elements must not nest (invalid HTML, breaks hydration, and is a real screen-reader/keyboard-nav hazard — a nested button is unreachable or ambiguous via assistive tech and violates the project's stated minimum web UX baseline for keyboard-operable, programmatically-labeled controls). The "Practice" row and "Delete goal" affordance need to be siblings, not parent/child.
- Console/network evidence: Full React nesting-violation stack trace captured, pointing at `GoalRow` — reproducible on every goals-list render (not a one-off).

### [severity: medium] Multi-trick sesh selection screen: sticky rank-section header visually overlaps last item(s) of the previous section during scroll

- What I did: Sesh → "Practice several tricks" → scrolled the trick list (both via UI wheel/JS-driven scroll) to reach custom tricks/sequence beneath "Focused tricks."
- What happened: While scrolled to certain offsets, the yellow "Rank 1" (or next-section) header band visually overlapped/obscured the second and third rows of the "Focused tricks" section (e.g., "ZZZ QA Unranked Trick" and "ZZZ QA Test Sequence" rows, with the "CUSTOM SEQUENCE" label partly hidden under the incoming header bar). The underlying DOM/accessibility tree content was intact (confirmed via `read_page`/`get_page_text`), and checkboxes were still clickable by reference, so this looks like a purely visual sticky-header layering issue, not a data or interaction bug.
- What should happen: Section headers should not visually overlap the content of the previous section during scroll; if this is an intentional sticky-header design, it needs a solid background and correct z-order/margin so trailing item text in the prior section is never obscured.
- Console/network evidence: None (visual-only). Screenshots taken during the scroll show the overlap.

### [severity: low] Goal-creation form shows a validation error before any user input

- What I did: Tricks → Goals for Big cup → Add goal → observed the "Target lace count" field immediately after the form opened, before typing anything.
- What happened: The field already displayed "Enter a whole number of laces from 1 to 100000." in red, as if the (empty) field had already been validated and failed.
- What should happen: Validation errors should generally appear after a user has interacted with (blurred/submitted) a field, not on initial render of an empty required field — this is a common a11y/UX smell (premature/alarming error state) even though it didn't block saving once a valid value was entered.
- Console/network evidence: None; purely a visual/UX observation.

### [severity: low] Deleting a custom trick referenced by a sequence step gives no warning about that reference

- What I did: Deleted "ZZZ QA Standalone Trick RENAMED," which was step 02 of "ZZZ QA Test Sequence."
- What happened: The delete confirmation dialog only said "It will leave your trick library and current practice list. Completed Sesh records and Stats history will stay intact." No mention that the trick is currently referenced as a step in a sequence, even though the app knows this at delete time.
- What should happen: This is not a correctness bug — per AGENTS.md, the sequence step's name snapshot is authoritative and deletion behaved correctly (confirmed: the sequence still rendered "ZZZ QA Standalone Trick" as step 02 afterward, no crash, no console errors). But a short heads-up ("also used in 1 sequence") would be a reasonable UX improvement so users aren't surprised later when the sequence step no longer resolves to a live, editable trick.
- Console/network evidence: None — noted for product/UX consideration only.

### [severity: low] AGENTS.md describes Profile as "intentionally only a placeholder," but the live Profile route has substantial functionality

- What I did: Navigated to `/app/profile`.
- What happened: The page includes: assessed Kentei rank display, Account (Sign up / Sign in), a full "Kentei assessment" entry point ("Run a real, timed assessment..."), app theme customization (background/accent/text color pickers with contrast ratios), a "Daily streak" minutes setting, and a feedback link.
- What should happen: This isn't a functional bug — nothing crashed and the trick directory is correctly absent from Profile (matching the one specific rule AGENTS.md calls out: "do not restore the trick directory there"). But calling the route "intentionally only a placeholder" undersells what's actually there and could mislead a future contributor reading AGENTS.md into thinking Profile has no real logic yet. Worth a documentation touch-up to describe current Profile scope accurately.
- Console/network evidence: None — documentation-accuracy observation only.

### [severity: low] Page `<title>` becomes empty immediately after some in-app custom-trick form submissions

- What I did: Observed `get_page_text`'s reported page title while working through the "Define new trick" → standalone-trick save flow.
- What happened: Right after saving a new standalone/unranked custom trick, one `get_page_text` read reported an empty `Title:` line (`Title: ` with nothing after it) before the Tricks list re-rendered. This did not reproduce consistently on every save and the browser tab title elsewhere correctly showed "Daily Dama: Tricks."
- What should happen: Route title should remain stable ("Daily Dama: Tricks") through client-side form-submission transitions. Low confidence/severity since it did not reproduce reliably and had no user-visible effect I could confirm in a screenshot — flagging for awareness rather than as a confirmed defect.
- Console/network evidence: None.

## Things that worked correctly

- **Home**: First-visit empty state ("Start your first sesh") rendered correctly; after completing seshes, "Pick up where you left off" and the Kentei "next up" card updated correctly; Stats widget on Home matched the dedicated Stats page.
- **Tricks — custom-trick creation**: Standalone ranked trick sorted correctly into its rank section, after all standard tricks at that rank (confirmed rank 3, appeared as item 06 after 5 standard tricks). Unranked custom trick correctly appeared only in the final "Custom · YOUR OWN PROGRESSION" section. Rank picker is a visual radio-style control with both a checkmark and a text label (not color-only) confirming selection.
- **Tricks — sequences**: Created a 3-step sequence mixing a custom-text step, a standalone-custom-trick step, and a standard-trick step. Sequence creation UI explicitly states "Sequences cannot be nested," and this was verified two ways: (1) searching the existing-trick candidate list by the exact name of an already-created sequence returned zero results, and (2) the candidate list only ever surfaced standard tricks and standalone custom tricks, never sequences — consistent with `selectSequenceStepCandidates` excluding sequences.
- **Tricks — name-snapshot invariant (the most important thing tested)**: Renamed a custom trick referenced by a sequence step; the sequence's step still showed the *old* name both in the edit form and in the active Sesh countdown/practice views. Then deleted that same trick entirely; the sequence still rendered the step's stored snapshot name with no crash and no console errors. This is exactly the AGENTS.md-documented invariant, and it held.
- **Tricks — goals and notes**: Empty states, add/save flows, and count badges ("NOTES — 1", "GOALS — 1") all worked and stayed in sync across the per-trick view, the aggregate "Trick goals"/"All notes" views, and after a full page reload.
- **Sesh — open-ended multi-trick**: Selecting multiple tricks (including a custom sequence) showed a live "N tricks selected" count; the sequence rendered its 3 steps as plain name strings in both the "Get ready" and active-practice screens (no live query against the custom-trick store — confirmed since one of its steps referenced an already-deleted/renamed trick and still displayed the frozen snapshot correctly); lace tapping, elapsed-time tracking, and the completion summary (per-trick laces/time) all worked.
- **Sesh — timed, early end**: Countdown ran, "Time remaining" counted down correctly, and ending early recorded actual elapsed time (00:26) rather than the full configured duration (01:00) or zero — matching the AGENTS.md rule that an early end records actual elapsed practice time. A nice touch: the trick's saved note ("From your notes") surfaced during active practice, and a "First lace ever" celebration banner appeared on completion.
- **Stats**: Total laces, practice time, and sesh count matched the completed practice-history records exactly (4 laces, ~01:16 total across three per-trick history rows); per-trick breakdown ("By trick") matched individual sesh summaries.
- **Profile**: Correctly does not include a trick directory/browsing UI (see documentation note above about scope beyond "placeholder").
- **Persistence/hydration**: Full hard reloads (via `location.reload()`) on both Home and Tricks preserved all custom tricks, sequences, lace counts, notes, and goals with no visible flash of empty/partially-hydrated UI in the screenshots taken immediately after reload.
- **Layout**: The five-link web nav (Home, Sesh, Tricks, Stats, Profile) fit correctly at a 320px viewport width with no wrapping or truncation.
- **Known-benign warnings**: Only the two documented benign warnings (`useNativeDriver` fallback, styled-components/native `@media (min-width` inline-style warning) appeared outside of the new nested-button hydration errors called out above — no other unexpected console errors were observed during normal navigation.
- **Keyboard/focus plumbing**: DOM inspection confirmed interactive controls (Practice, Delete, checkboxes) consistently use `role="button"`/`role="checkbox"` with `tabIndex={0}` and descriptive `aria-label`s (e.g., "Practice Big cup," "Select ZZZ QA Unranked Trick," "Delete goal") — the programmatic-labeling half of the minimum web UX baseline looked solid everywhere I inspected it, though live Tab-key traversal could not be fully exercised through this automation harness (focus-via-JS worked; synthesized keyboard Tab events did not reliably move `document.activeElement` in this environment, which looks like a harness limitation rather than an app defect, but was not independently confirmed either way).

# T319 plan: readable foregrounds for accent-filled surfaces

Branch `task/T319-derive-readable-foregrounds-for-accent`. No blockers: `deriveReadableForeground` (T283) already exists in `src/features/profile/state/use-resolved-theme.ts` and is the pattern to extend. No persisted-schema, provider or state change; theme is derived at render time.

## Root cause (verified in code)
`Hero` (daily-home.tsx:259), `CelebrationBannerCard`, `NextTrickButton`, `MultiTrickAction` (sesh.tsx ~1383-1400) fill with `theme.accent`, but their text uses `theme.text`, `theme.textMuted` (Eyebrow/BodyText in `src/components/ui/typography.tsx`) or `AppColors.inkSoft`. `theme.text` is derived against background AND accent jointly (min of both), and `textMuted` is a 65/35 blend toward the page background, so on Red (#c0392b accent, #fbdcd6 bg) both are weak on the actual surface.

## Steps (in order)
1. **Theme model** (`use-resolved-theme.ts`, test-first in `use-resolved-theme.test.ts`):
   - Add to `ResolvedTheme`: `onAccent` (primary text on accent fills) and `onAccentMuted` (secondary copy on accent fills).
   - `onAccent = deriveReadableForeground(accent, text)`. This keeps the user's/auto `text` (hierarchy and custom text override respected) when it already reaches 4.5:1 on the accent, otherwise cream/ink whichever is higher. Max(cream, ink) clears 4.5 on any surface; the test sweep proves it.
   - New pure `deriveMutedOnSurface(foreground, surface, minRatio = 4.5)`: try `blendHexColors(foreground, surface, w)` for w = 0.8 (muted look, less aggressive than the 0.65 page blend), 0.9, then return `foreground`; take the first meeting `minRatio`. Chosen target for secondary copy: 4.5:1 (it is body-size copy on a hero/button, not decorative). Check `blendHexColors` arg order/semantic in `src/shared/color/blend.ts` (existing call is `blendHexColors(text, background, 0.65)`, where 0.65 is the text weight).
   - Wire both into `resolveTheme`. Do not touch `accent`, `text`, `textMuted` or saved preferences.

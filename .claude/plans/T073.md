# T073 — Keep “Next trick” available with auto-advance enabled

## Good enough for this iteration

For an active non-final trick, `Next trick` is rendered and manually advances the run even when `advanceOnFirstLace` is enabled and the current trick has no laces. A lace on an auto-advance run still records the landing and transitions automatically, while the final active trick still has no redundant `Next trick` action. No changes are made to persistence, the Sesh reducer, countdown behavior, or mobile validation.

## Notes and assumptions

- The shared `MultiTrickSesh` behavior applies to callers such as the Kentei assessment flow as well as ordinary multi-trick Seshes. T073’s explicit requirement supersedes the earlier T057 suppression: non-final active tricks expose the manual escape hatch even when first-lace auto-advance is enabled.
- The manual action should continue to dispatch `seshEnded` rather than introduce a new reducer action or direct sequence mutation. This preserves recording and stale-result protection already centralized in the completed-state effect.
